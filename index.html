The Real Pain Story

Real Pain has always been more than just ink on skin,
it's a canvas for stories, growth, and genuine connection.

The Real Pain Tattoo Studio started with two friends who both love tattoos and are good at art. One of them really liked tattoos and often looked at other people's tattoos because he was amazed by how creative and detailed, they were. The other one was good at making designs using pencil and paper. One day, while they were just talking, the tattoo lover encouraged his friend to try using his talent as a job. That simple conversation led to the start of Real Pain Tattoo Studio in 2021. What began as a small effort with strong passion and dedication is now becoming a place for people passionate about tattoos.
At Real Pain, every tattoo is a masterpiece!
